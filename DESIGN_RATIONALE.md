# Design Rationale & Recruiter Critique

## 1. Core Thesis: The Website as the First Product Artefact

The objective of this portfolio is not to copy a traditional CV into HTML, but to provide undeniable proof of **Product Thinking** from the first 5 seconds of interaction.

For someone transitioning from Senior Recruitment / Talent Advisory to Product Management, the most common trap is either:
1. Pretending to have already been a Senior PM (which hiring managers detect immediately and reject), or
2. Presenting a conventional chronological resume (which gets pigeonholed as HR/recruitment only).

This website solves that dilemma by:
- **Translating transferable strengths** into rigorous product competencies (Stakeholder discovery, problem framing, trade-off prioritization, and telemetry).
- **Positioning evidence first**: Using the **11-step Case Study Framework** to show how Caroline deconstructs operational bottlenecks into structured solutions.
- **Introducing the Signature Interactive Device**: The 4-stage *Observe ➔ Frame ➔ Explore ➔ Decide* interactive mental model immediately signals structured reasoning.

## 2. Recruiter Critique & Persona Evaluations

### Perspective 1: The Product Recruiter (10-Second Scan)
- **What is understood in 10 seconds?** Caroline is a bilingual London-based operator with 4+ years of real-world experience at Lawrence Harvey, Emerique & Partners, and Ecolab, intentionally moving into Product.
- **What feels credible?** The fact that she doesn't fabricate a "Chief Product Officer" title; her employment history is verifiable, and her narrative frames her experience as an asset for discovery and stakeholder management.
- **Action triggered**: High likelihood of bookmarking and reaching out for Associate / Junior / Operations-heavy Product roles.

### Perspective 2: The Hiring Manager (3-Minute Evaluation)
- **What feels credible?** The **Experience-to-Product Bridge** and the **Product Lens** annotations on each role. The hiring manager sees that Caroline understands the difference between symptoms and root causes.
- **What makes them continue reading?** The flagship case study on *Feedback Loop Latency*. Seeing candidate drop-off treated as a conversion funnel metric demonstrates immediate business acumen.

### Perspective 3: The Senior Product Manager (Deep Dive)
- **What feels credible?** The 11-step case study structure: Context, Assumptions Tested, Stakeholder Constraints, Options Evaluated, and Success Metrics.
- **Key takeaway**: Caroline thinks in terms of trade-offs and feedback loops rather than feature checklists.

## 3. Creative Direction: The Editorial Product Notebook

- **Visual Tone**: Intelligent, warm, structured, and confident.
- **Palette**: Warm cream paper background (`#FAF8F5`), charcoal ink (`#15181C`), terracotta accent (`#C85A32`), and sage green (`#2F6B52`).
- **Typography Pairing**: Editorial Serif (`Newsreader`) for thoughtful display headlines paired with high-legibility sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`) and monospace (`JetBrains Mono`) for technical metadata and annotations.
